About

I like building things that work.

I'm an engineer who spent five years taking tires from prototype to production at Michelin, across plants in China, India and Thailand. I'm now finishing Kellogg's MMM dual degree (MBA and MS in Design Innovation) in Chicago.

I care about human-centered design, product innovation and solving messy operational problems. Whether it's taking a physical product from 0 to 1, fixing a supply chain or building sustainability into a product's life cycle, I like solutions that scale.

Over the last two years I've also been building software with AI as a coding partner: a RAG over my course material, a funding database for an accelerator, prototypes for class clients, and this site. AI lets an engineer like me ship real tools fast, and I use it every week.

Before all that, I studied mechanical engineering at BITS Pilani, where I led aerodynamics and fundraising for our Formula Student team and won the reverse-engineering event.

Talk to me about: new product introduction, sustainable product development, product design, operations strategy, and building with AI.

Heavy-Industry NPI Toolkit

A stage-gate toolkit and $15M+ savings roadmap for a heavy industrial manufacturer's product development.

Year2025
RoleSummer Associate
ContextKearney, Product Excellence and Renewal Lab
ClientA heavy industrial manufacturer
13+product lines on shared platforms
~5,000change orders analyzed
$15M+savings identified
500engineers surveyed

01Problem

The client's new product introduction process ran two years and every product line planned proposals its own way. Late engineering changes were costly, and leaders couldn't see which skills their teams were missing.

02What I did

  • Built the stage-gate toolkit for the two-year NPI process, using a seven-level engineering systems model. It standardized platforms to speed proposal planning across 13+ product lines on six platforms.
  • Analyzed five years of manufacturing change orders, about 5,000 records. I clustered root causes by type and department and showed that 85% traced to a short list of high-impact fixes, worth $15M+ in savings.
  • Built a skills matrix from a 500-person, 90-question survey across six departments, benchmarking current against needed skills to target upskilling.

03Why it matters to me

This was Michelin's industrialization problems from the other side of the table. The pattern is the same: most cost is decided early, and good data on where things go wrong pays for itself.

Sherpa: AI Trip Packing

An AI gear companion for an outdoor-gear brand that turns trip planning from stress into anticipation.

Year2025
RoleProduct designer and prototyper
ContextInnovation sprint, team of 5
ClientA premium outdoor-gear brand
5in-depth user interviews
3live pilot trips
2prototype rounds
5steps from idea to departure

01Problem

Outdoor people love the adventure and hate the packing. They pull lists from blogs, Reddit and old spreadsheets, text friends about shared gear, then still forget things or overpack. Excitement turns into anxiety before the trip even starts. For a gear brand, that moment before the trip is where it can earn trust beyond the products themselves.

02How I thought about it

We mapped one adventurer's journey before, during and after a trip. The pain clustered in planning and packing, so that became our opportunity zone. Our research question was simple: would people trust an AI assistant to plan their packing list?

The first prototype was a rule-based chat flow. Users liked that it felt like an expert guide, but said it asked too many questions and missed the ones that mattered. The biggest lesson: the value isn't the list, it's helping people reason about their trip.

"It guided me like an expert outdoor guide, and the questions made it fun and personalized."

03What we built

We rebuilt it as a working web app with an AI app builder, around three principles: smarter not more, personalization is key, and the path of least resistance.

  • Personalized gear lists from destination, real-time weather, duration and activity.
  • Weight and space optimization, which testers called out as a favorite.
  • Group coordination that splits shared gear and removes duplicates.
  • Context-aware product suggestions that feel helpful, not pushy.

04Roadmap

Phase one is a minimum lovable product: an insight engine, a context-aware list and an editable checklist. Phase two adds group planning, wearable integrations and route suggestions. We decided to pivot on shareability, persevere on balancing general needs with brand products, and fix the experience for beginners.

Employee Pulse

One app for frontline hotel staff to connect, grow and look after themselves.

Year2024
ContextKellogg MMM, team of 6
RoleResearcher and concept designer
ClientA global hotel group
12people interviewed across roles
3core employee needs
1single access point

01Problem

How might a global hotel group improve the experience of its on-property employees through innovation? Front desk, events and operations staff carry the brand, but they're spread across properties, shifts and franchise owners.

02How I thought about it

We interviewed front desk staff, managers, interns and corporate experience designers, and read what associates said online. Three needs came up again and again:

  • Connection: culture and team fit are why people join and why they leave.
  • Development: promotion paths felt unclear. One associate waited eight years for a promotion.
  • Wellbeing: flexibility, support for the physical side of the job and a safe place to raise issues.

03What we designed

  • Interest-based channels that work as low-effort resource groups.
  • A dashboard each associate customizes around their needs.
  • Kudos and recognition between peers and managers.
  • Career guidance filtered by hotel tier and job type.
  • Tiered access and search across the property, the city and the global network.

04Rollout

We planned a phased rollout with local champions, feedback loops run by the employee experience team, and integration with existing systems.

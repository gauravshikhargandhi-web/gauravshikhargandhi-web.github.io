From Program to Platform

A design-thinking workshop that turned a vague "be great" brief into a clear vision and three options for leadership to choose from.

ClientA corporate-backed climate accelerator

01Problem

The program team and its corporate sponsor didn't agree on what success looked like, and founders felt support stopped when the program ended. The only brief was to be great.

02What we did

  1. Listened: ran a working session with the team and sponsor, and captured what people said in their own words.
  2. Framed: clustered the discussion into 10 How Might We questions.
  3. Ideated: generated 50+ ideas and scored each for impact.
  4. Prioritized: turned the strongest ideas into clear asks, each with a crawl, walk and run step.

03Outcome

A written vision to take the program from one cohort a year to a year-round platform, with three options from keeping what works to building it all. Leadership now has something concrete to react to and a decision to make, instead of an open-ended brief.

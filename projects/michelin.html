Michelin: Prototype to Production

Five years taking B2B tires from concept to volume production across China, India and Thailand.

01Industrialization

I owned launch execution and product quality for two B2B portfolios across three plants in Asia Pacific. I drove more than ten product lines from prototype to volume production of 100,000 units a year, about $50M in annual revenue.

  • Raised first-pass yield (Right First Time) to a record 95%+, up from 80%, by rebuilding and deploying two digital automation tools used by 100+ engineers across five teams.
  • Cut prototype-to-mass-production time by 75%, from two years to six months, on a four-line transfer from China to Thailand. The fix was aligning product development and manufacturing on shared KPIs and one launch plan.

02Concept design and sustainability

As a concept product design analyst I owned two B2B products end to end, from concept to launch, and made go or no-go calls against decarbonization targets.

  • Developed and tested a life cycle assessment (LCA) tool that contributed to a 30% cut in Scope 1 and 2 emissions and a 30% rise in the sustainable-material rate.
  • Ran structured experiments that improved rolling resistance, mileage and wear by 10 to 30%.

03Tools I built for engineers

  • A decision-support design tool and framework that cut prototype model lead time by 80%, adopted by 12 engineers across about 50 products a year.
  • An analytical performance guide that replaced day-long simulations with fast analytical factors, so engineers could compare technology trade-offs live in design reviews.

Building tools for other engineers is where I first got hooked on making software. It's the same instinct behind my AI projects today.

04Public context

Michelin's public reporting describes the programs this work fed into:

  • Michelin's 360° environmental approach: LCA across 16 impacts, applied to every new project since 2023.
  • Michelin aiming for 100% sustainable tyres: 40% sustainable materials by 2030 and 100% by 2050.
  • A sustainable path to mining tyre design: how LCA shapes B2B tire design, where 70 to 90% of impact happens in use.

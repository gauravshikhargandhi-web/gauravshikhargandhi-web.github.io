Collective: Bulk for City Renters

Fractional bulk delivery that brings warehouse-club prices to renters without a car or a storeroom.

ContextKellogg MMM, team of 6
RoleStrategy and business case
ClientA warehouse-club retailer
Ask$1.5M, 120-day pilot
42%of U.S. renters lack a personal vehicle
18%higher grocery bills in cities
20%markup paid for mini sizes
120days to de-risk and launch

01Problem

Warehouse clubs assume a car and a garage. City renters have neither, so they pay rideshare costs or a 20% markup on small sizes at corner stores. Meanwhile, apartment managers need digital amenities to attract and keep residents.

02What we proposed

  1. Order through the building: residents see available bulk items in their apartment's resident portal.
  2. Split the bulk: a predictive cart pools neighbors' orders and splits bulk packs into the sizes people asked for.
  3. One delivery: the retailer delivers once to the building.
  4. Locker pickup: staff store orders in a dedicated locker and notify residents.

03Business case

We framed it as a pitch: opportunity, solution, benefit, ability, ask. The model fills urban white space without new warehouses, adds apartment-level data, and uses off-peak, labor-efficient fulfillment. We asked for $1.5M and a cross-functional team to validate it in 120 days.
